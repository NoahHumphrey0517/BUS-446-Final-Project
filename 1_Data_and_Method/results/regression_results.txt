Simple Linear Regression
X = Year, Y = Average age of first-time homebuyers (2000-2025, n = 26)

Equation:   Age = 191.36 + (-0.0769 x Year)
Slope:      -0.0769 years per year  (about -0.77 years per decade)
R-squared:  0.576
p-value:    0.000007

Interpretation: Each year is associated with first-time buyers being about
0.08 years younger on average. Year explains about
58% of the year-to-year variation in average first-time buyer age.
Because p < 0.05, the relationship is statistically significant.
